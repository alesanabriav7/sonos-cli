# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, evidence, and observable behavior.
- Protect other people's privacy and remove device identifiers from reports.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about safety boundaries and scope.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, and other
unwelcome conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. The
maintainer may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
